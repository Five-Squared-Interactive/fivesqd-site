# A world your friends meet in (/doors; /builders forwards here)

Named by what it shows, never by who it is for (founder, 2026-11-11). For
people who want a world of their own, with other people in it. The fix
shown; what works today. No fear-led opening and no then-and-now framing
(review, 2026-11-11). `[slot]` marks pictures only the founder can make.
This file is the words twin of `src/pages/Builders.jsx`; keep them in step.

## Hero

**A world of your own, with your friends in it.**
Your world is a few plain files at its own web address. Your people walk
in from a link, and a door in it points to other worlds. You don't run a
server.
[Explore worlds on WorldHub](https://www.worldhub.me/) · [Build one from Blender](/first-world)
*(Recording: the same loop as the home page, `public/video/hero-2026-11-13d.webm`;
the door clip waits for v4-monorepo #873.)*

## Your people, in your world, without running a server.

Whoever opens the link is in the world with everyone else there at that
moment. You see each other move and you can chat. The visitors' browsers
connect to each other, so nothing of yours has to stay switched on.
*(Picture: a second visitor in the loft's hall with a name over their
head, `loft-two-visitors.webp`; the name is the one the world gives an
anonymous visitor.)*

## Put a door in your world that points to a friend's.

A door in your world names a friend's world at its own address, the way
one page links to the next. Each world's own file says where its doors
lead.
*(Picture: the loft's front door at the end of its hall, `loft-door.webp`;
the clip through it waits for #873, and the copy says a door "names" a
friend's world.)*

## What you get

- Build in Blender, publish in one press, send the link.
- People together in the world, on phones, laptops and headsets.
- Doors between worlds; a door is a field on the object in Blender.
- Choose who may enter: everyone with the link, or only the people you name.

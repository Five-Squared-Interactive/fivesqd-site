## Hero

Kicker: World builders.
**Your world lives on someone else's terms.**
Platforms change the rules, ban accounts, shut down, and years of
building go with them. Making your own meant a game engine and a server
to keep running. Here a world is yours: a few files at your own address,
with your people in it, and no server to run.

## Your people, in your world, without running a server.

Whoever opens the link is there with everyone else who has it. You see
each other move and you can talk. The visitors' browsers talk to each
other directly; nothing of yours has to stay switched on.
[slot: a group of visitors with name labels, a door in the background]

## Doors to other worlds. Yours and your friends'.

A door in your world can open onto a friend's world at their own address,
the way one page links to the next. Nobody owns the map, and nobody can
take your world off it.
[slot: three seconds through a door, the address bar changing]

## What works today, and what does not yet

Today: build in Blender, publish in one press, send the link · people
together in the world, on phones, laptops and headsets · doors between
worlds; a door is a field on the object in Blender · choose who may
enter · a world's own logic (a score, a simulation, live terrain) runs on
an open runtime, WorldOS, for the few worlds that need one.
Next: building inside the world, from the browser, without Blender ·
scripting a world's behaviour from the add-on (the format supports it;
the authoring tool is coming) · downloading your world's folder to host
anywhere.

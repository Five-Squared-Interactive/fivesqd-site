# Five-second headline test, 2026-10-24

Four headlines on the real first screen (desktop and phone), read fresh
as five personas: a Blender hobbyist, an archviz designer, a friend who
got a link in a group chat, a sceptical tech journalist, a developer.

| Headline | Primary (makers) | A stranger can say what it does |
|---|---|---|
| A. Send a place, not a picture. | 6 | 4 |
| B. Your Blender scene, on your friend's phone, in one press. | 7.5 | 8 |
| C. Your 3D scene, as a place people can visit. | 5 | 6 |
| D. Walk your friends through the world you built. | 6.5 | 5 |

Findings beyond the headline: "You made a space in Blender" assumes a
maker; "world" is the metaverse trigger for the journalist and the
developer; "Nothing to install" reads as an overclaim next to a Blender
button (say "nothing for visitors to install"); "together" appeared only
in the placeholder's brief; the company name was missing on the phone
header; "Make a world" appeared three times on one screen.

Recommendation (a write-in): **"Your Blender scene, as a link friends can
walk into."** with "Press Save and Publish in Blender and you get a web
address. Anyone who opens it walks around inside, on a phone or a laptop,
together with whoever else is there. Nothing for them to install." It
costs the archviz reader ("friends" reads hobby-grade), who converts from
the clip and a later walkthrough line instead.

Applied: that line and subhead on the Blender page (/first-world). The
home page, which is the company's and written from the visitor's side,
uses "A place you can walk into, from a link." with "together", "no app"
and ownership in the subhead, and "world" kept out of the first screen.
The other fixes (phone header name, one Make a world, "nothing for
visitors to install") are in.

## Round two (motive-led lines), same five personas

| Headline | Makers | Stranger | Meets them where they are |
|---|---|---|---|
| E. Let people stand in what you made. | 8 | 6 | 8 |
| F. Your friend opens a link and is standing in the room you built. | 6 | 9 | 7 |
| G. Meet your friends inside the place you made. | 4 | 4 | 5 |
| B. Your Blender scene, as a link friends can walk into. | 8 | 7 | 6 |

The reviewer recommended E; the founder had already called E too vague,
so F is live, with a subhead that no longer echoes it (model, press,
send; they walk around it on their phone, with you). Taken from the
round: the nav button no longer says "world" ("Start in Blender");
"nothing for them to install"; G's social register is out. Open: "Your
friend" costs the archviz reader, who sends to clients.

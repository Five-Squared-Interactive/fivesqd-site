## A planet is a world too

The same client that opens a living room opens a planet: terrain that goes
on for thousands of kilometres, oceans, weather, a horizon that curves.
You can fly down from orbit and land, walk, and find a door that leads
back into a room. It is the same kind of document; it just describes a
sphere.

[Walk onto one]({PLANET_WORLD_URL})

## How a document holds a planet

A planet's document names the sphere and the rules for its surface, and
the surface arrives in tiles as you approach, baked ahead of time by a
generator and served as files like everything else. Up close, the ground
is made of blocks you can change, and those changes are kept by a runtime
on WorldOS, the one case where a world has a server of its own.

## Worlds within worlds

A planet can sit inside a larger world, a solar system, and other worlds
can sit on a planet: a house at a latitude and longitude, a town, a room
you exported from Blender. Each is its own document at its own address.
A world can place another world the way a page embeds another page, and
visitors walk between them without a load screen.

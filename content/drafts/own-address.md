## Your folder, your address

A world is a folder: `index.veml` and the models it names. Put that folder
on any web host, your own domain, GitHub Pages, a bucket, and the world is
at that address. Nothing about it points at us.

## Opening it

A link to a world at your own address opens the client pointed at your
folder: `{CLIENT_URL}#world-url=https://your.site/world/index.veml`. The
client is a web page; your host serves the world, the client's host serves
the client, and the two never need to be the same.

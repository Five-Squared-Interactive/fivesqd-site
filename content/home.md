## Three things people do with it (no heading on the page)

Three cards, each a picture slot, the thing in one line, how in one line,
"How →" to the page for it:

- **Walk a client through the kitchen before it's built.** Send a private
  link. They open it on their phone and you're in there with them.
  (/architects)
- **Send a friend the room you modelled, not a render of it.** Press Save
  and Publish in Blender. Your scene becomes a link people walk through.
  (/first-world)
- **Make a world your friends meet in, at its own address.** Put doors in
  it to other worlds. You don't run a server. (/builders)

## From a Blender scene to a link

1. **Install the add-on.** Blender 4.5 or newer. Sign in once with Google,
   GitHub or an emailed link. [slot: the add-on panel, cropped at 2x]
2. **Press Save and Publish.** Your scene becomes a world at its own web
   address and opens in your browser. [slot: the link the add-on shows]
3. **Send the link.** Paste it in a message. Whoever taps it walks in. Let
   in everyone, or only the people you name. [slot: the world on a phone,
   in a hand, both sticks showing]

## Free, and open

The software is free and open source under MIT. Publishing to WorldHub,
our hosting service, costs nothing. Worlds are hosted at their own web
address, people are in them together on phones and laptops, and doors
lead between them.

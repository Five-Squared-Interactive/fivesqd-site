## Three things people do with it (no heading on the page)

Three cards, each a picture slot, the thing in one line, how in one line,
"How →" to the page for it:

- **Walk a client through the kitchen before it's built.** Send a private
  link. They open it on their phone and you're in there with them.
  (/architects)
- **Send a friend the room you modelled, not a render of it.** Press Save
  and Publish in Blender. Your scene becomes a link people walk through.
  (/first-world)
- **Make a world your friends meet in, at its own address.** Put doors in
  it to other worlds. You don't run a server. (/builders)

## A world is a web page.

Here is everything in a published place: one small text file, one model,
one sky. The format is open and the files are plain, so reading a world
never depends on us. *(The loft, walkable, beside the loft's own
document, 787 bytes, with the five marks: its name, the room, the sky,
where you start, the model.)*

## Free, and open

The software is free and open source under MIT. Publishing to WorldHub,
our hosting service, costs nothing. Every world lives at its own web
address, and a door in one world can lead into another.

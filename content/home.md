# Home

Founder, 2026-11-12: the home page says one thing, that making a good
shared world is easy, and it says it the way a product site does: one line
per section with the picture it needs. No button names, field names or
mechanics on it ("we are describing nitty gritty things that makes it
sound complicated"); nobody labelled by who they are; Blender is one of
the tools you already use, not the subject ("the home page is solely
focused on the blender plugin"); the loft once, at the same size as the
other pictures ("too much weight on the loft when it isn't that
impressive"). `[slot]` marks a picture or recording only the founder can
make; `[ph]` a fact he must supply. This file is the words twin of
`src/pages/Home.jsx`; keep them in step.

## Hero (text left, recording right)

**Easy 3D. Instant. Shared. On any device.** (the founder's four words)

Make a place, send the link, and people walk around in it with you.

[Walk into one now]({DEMO_WORLD_URL}: the loft) · [Make one](/first-world)

Free and open source.

## Share the worlds you already have.

Bring a scene from the tools you already use. It comes in as it is.
*(Review, 2026-11-13: "Nothing to relearn, nothing to rebuild" was the
third "nothing to…" on the page.)*
*(Picture: the subway station's hall, `station-hall.webp`: a model made
elsewhere, brought into Blender and published unchanged. A credit line
under the picture: "Subway station" by Pasha, CC BY 4.0, with the
Sketchfab link.)*

## Open the link. You're there.

On a phone, a laptop or in VR, in the browser. Nothing for your visitors
to install.
*(The loft frame, click to walk in, the same size as the other pictures.)*

## Together, from the start.

Whoever opens the link is in there with you.
*(Picture: a second visitor in the loft's hall with a name over their
head, `loft-two-visitors.webp`. The name is the one the world gives an
anonymous visitor, so the copy says "a name", never "their name".)*

## Make it, send it.

From a scene to a link in one press. Paste it anywhere people read.
*(Picture: the Save as My World box after one press, World Address filled
in; the station's panel, shot 2026-11-13.)*

## Private when you want.

Only the people you name get in.
*(Picture: the Save as My World box with Only People I Allow ticked and
two example viewers, `blender-only-people-i-allow.webp`; the kitchen on a
phone showed anonymous visitors, the opposite of the claim, so it moved
to the private-link page.)*

## Worlds lead to worlds.

A door in your world names someone else's, at its own address, the way
pages link to pages.
*(Picture: the loft's front door at the end of its hall, `loft-door.webp`,
whose link names the station; the clip through it waits for v4-monorepo
#873, since today the door opens an empty world, and the copy says
"names", never "step through".)*

## It's yours.

Plain files at your own address, in an open format, on any host you like.
[How it works](/technology)
*(The loft's whole document, 1,119 bytes since the door went in on
2026-11-13, with six marks, as the picture; no second loft frame. Its
caption, word for word: "A whole world, with a door in it: 1,119 bytes.
Add /index.veml to any published world's address and read it.")*

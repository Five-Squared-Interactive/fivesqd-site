# About Five Squared Interactive

We build the pieces that let a 3D world be a web page: a document at an
address, opened in a browser, linked to other worlds, and owned by whoever
made it.

## What we believe

A world should outlive the company that helped make it. So the format is
open, the client is open, hosting is a convenience and never a requirement,
and the design puts nothing of ours between a link and the world it
points to. A
thousand worlds on our servers would mean less to us than one person's
world at their own address.

## What we make

The open stack: VEML, WebVerse, StraightFour, WorldSync, WorldOS, and the
authoring tools under the WorldKit name. One service, WorldHub, which hosts
what people publish from Blender and signs them in. [What we make](/products)

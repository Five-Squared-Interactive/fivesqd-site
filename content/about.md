An independent studio building the pieces that let a 3D world be a web
page: a set of files at an address, opened in a browser, linked to other
worlds, and owned by whoever made it.

## What we believe

A world should outlive the company that helped make it. So the format is
open, the page that opens worlds is open, hosting is a convenience the
design never requires, and the design puts nothing of ours between a link
and the world it points to. A thousand worlds on our servers would mean
less to us than one person's world at their own address.

## How we got here

  link. Today a Blender user publishes a scene as a world in one press,

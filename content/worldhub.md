# WorldHub

The service that hosts what you publish, signs you in, and lists what you
want found. Run by Five Squared Interactive.

## What it does

**Hosts your published worlds.** Press Save as My World in Blender and the
world is served at `worlds.worldhub.me/w/…`, with nothing for you to set
up. Publish again and the same link shows the new version.

**Signs you in.** WorldHub ID is the sign-in the add-on and the sites use:
Google, GitHub, or a link we email you. Visitors who are signed in appear
in a world under their own names.

**Lists worlds.** Search at `www.worldhub.me` is a directory of worlds
people have chosen to list, at any address, ours or not. A listing is a
link; the world stays where it is.

## What it does not do

It does not hold your world. A published world is a folder you can
download and put anywhere, and the link to a world at your own address
goes straight to it; WorldHub is not in the way. It does not decide what a
world may contain or do. It is not needed to visit, to make, or to share a
world. It is the easy path, not the only one.

## Cost

Publishing from Blender and visiting are free. There is no paid tier to
announce; when hosting at scale has a price, it will be here.

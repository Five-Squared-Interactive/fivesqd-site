# WorldHub

The service that hosts what you publish and signs you in. Run by Five
Squared Interactive.

## What it does

**Hosts your published worlds.** Press Save and Publish in Blender and the
world is served at `worlds.worldhub.me/w/…`, with nothing for you to set
up. Publish again and the same link shows the new version.

**Signs you in.** WorldHub ID is the sign-in the add-on and the sites use:
Google, GitHub, or a link we email you. A world you publish can be open to
everyone or only to the people you allow; visitors who are signed in appear
in a world under their own names.


## What it is not

A world does not need WorldHub to exist: a world at any other address is
served by that address. WorldHub does not
decide what a world may contain or do. It is the easy path, not the only
one.

## Cost

Publishing from Blender and visiting are free.

# Your first world

From a Blender scene to a link anyone can walk into.

## What you need

- Blender 4.5 or newer, free from blender.org.
- Node.js installed; the add-on uses it to build your world.
- The add-on: [download]({ADDON_URL}).

## Install the add-on

In Blender: Edit → Preferences → Add-ons. Open the menu at the top right
and choose Install from Disk…, then pick the file you downloaded. Tick
**VEML Import/Export** to turn it on. Already had an older version? Remove
it first, then install this one.

## Sign in once

In the add-on's preferences, press **Sign In**. Your browser opens
WorldHub; sign in with Google, GitHub, or a link we email you, and Blender
picks it up on its own. You stay signed in; there is a Sign Out beside it.

## Publish

Save your .blend file, then open the Scene tab of the Properties editor and
find the **VEML World** panel. Press **Save as My World**. The first time
takes a little longer. When it is done, your world opens in the browser.

Where do people start? Select a camera, open its Object tab, and tick
**VEML Entry Point** in the VEML Entity panel. Otherwise they start at the
scene's camera.

## Share

The address your world opened at is the one to share, for example
`https://worlds.worldhub.me/w/…`. Anyone can open it in a browser; nothing
to install. Press Save as My World again and the same address shows the new
version. Visitors who are signed in appear under their own names.

## Walk

Click in the world to look around with the mouse; Esc gives the mouse back.
W A S D to move, Space to jump. F to fly, where the world allows it: Space
up, Shift down. H shows these again. On a phone or tablet: drag to look,
and use the on-screen stick to move.

## Now look at what you made

Your world is a web page, and you own it.

- **Open the document.** Next to the world is a link to the file behind it.
  It is short. The title, the starting point, the sky, the model: all there
  in plain text.
- **Change something.** Edit the title or move the starting point, save,
  and the same link shows the change. *(Editing on the page itself is
  coming; until then, change it in Blender and publish again.)*
- **Take it with you.** Download the world's folder. Put it on any web
  host, your own domain included, and the link works from there. Nothing
  you made depends on us.
- **Link it to another world.** A door, a frame, a sign in your scene can
  open onto any other world's address. That is how one world becomes part
  of many.
